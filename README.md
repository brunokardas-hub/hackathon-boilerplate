# hackathon-boilerplate
🚀 Production-ready boilerplate for rapid hackathon development and high-performance econometric data analysis in 2026. Optimized for Cursor AI.

## TradingView terminal (`tradingview/terminal.pine`)

A Bloomberg-style dashboard for TradingView, written in Pine Script v6. It draws an amber-on-black panel over any chart:

- **Quote**: last price, net and % change, open, high, low, previous close, volume compared with its 20-day average
- **52-week range**: the high, the low and a bar showing where the price sits between them
- **Performance**: 1D / 1W / 1M / 3M / YTD / 1Y returns, plus relative strength against a benchmark (SPY by default)
- **Technicals**: RSI, ATR, MACD, three EMAs, VWAP and an overall trend read
- **Fundamentals** (stocks only): market cap, P/E, EPS, revenue and year-over-year revenue growth, free cash flow, margins, return on equity, debt-to-equity, dividend yield, the last earnings surprise and the next earnings date
- **Macro**: policy rate, inflation (CPI year-over-year), unemployment and quarterly GDP growth for a country you choose, each with its change since the previous release
- **Monitor**: six symbols you choose (default SPY, QQQ, DXY, US10Y, Gold, BTC)
- **Analyst note and score**: a plain-English summary of the move, trend, momentum, 52-week position, relative strength and upcoming earnings, plus a 0–100 score (shown in the Data Window, with alerts above 70 and below 30). It's rule-based arithmetic on the panel's numbers, not AI and not advice.
- **MARKET panel** (a second panel, bottom-left by default): a 4×3 heatmap coloured by % change, a breadth line with the day's leader and laggard, and a screener you can sort by % change, RSI or relative volume and filter to gainers, losers, oversold, overbought or unusual volume. Choose the universe: US sectors, US mega caps, crypto, global macro, or your own list of up to 12 tickers.
- **24/7 alerts**: alert conditions for your own price levels, prior-day high/low breaks, gaps, volume spikes, 52-week breaks, RSI, EMA crosses and the score, plus one bundled alert ("Any alert() function call") that covers them all. Setup: [docs/TRADINGVIEW_ALERTS.md](docs/TRADINGVIEW_ALERTS.md).
- **Overlays and alerts**: EMAs, VWAP and the prior day's high/low, plus alerts for 52-week breaks, RSI extremes and EMA crosses

**Install:** open TradingView → *Pine Editor* → paste in the contents of `terminal.pine` → *Save* → *Add to chart*. Change symbols, position and size in the indicator's settings.

## Web terminal (`web/index.html`)

A Bloomberg-style page in a single file. Use it at **https://brunokardas-hub.github.io/hackathon-boilerplate/** once GitHub Pages is on (see below), or download the file and double-click it.

- **Command line**: type `AAPL`, `NYSE:BA`, `BTCUSD` or `EURUSD` and press Enter to load a symbol. Other commands: `N`, `GP`, `FA`, `TA` and `ECO` jump to a panel; `HEAT` / `HEAT CRYPTO` open the market heatmap; `EQS` / `EQS CRYPTO` / `EQS FX` open the screener; `ASK <question>` asks the AI analyst; `BRIEF` writes a morning briefing; `HELP` lists everything. Press `/` to focus the command line.
- **TODAY** (opens first): market mood, today's risk against your rules, open trades (flagged when near the stop), the best A/B setups from the scanner with LOG buttons, My list's biggest movers, alerts nearest to firing, earnings this week for your stocks, your five-step process and quick actions. The site reopens on the screen you used last; keys 1–9 and 0 switch screens, and the command line suggests commands and your symbols as you type.
- **CHARTS**: a full-height chart with one-click timeframes (1m to 1W), chart styles (candles, Heikin Ashi, line, area) and indicator sets (trend, momentum, volatility), next to your list. Click a symbol to chart it.
- **My list**: a readable live table (big prices that flash on each tick, % change, a freshness dot) on MAIN and CHARTS. Add symbols in the box at the top; ✕ removes one. The MARKETS tab shows TradingView's multi-asset monitor.
- **⚙ SETTINGS**: both API keys, the state of every data source, and backup/restore in one place. First-time visitors get a short welcome card.
- **MAIN screen**: a large TradingView chart (press **⤢ MAX** or type `MAX` to fill the screen; Esc to exit), security overview, a multi-asset monitor, a technicals summary, company financials (stocks only), an economic calendar, a ticker tape and world clocks.
- **News**: works with no setup, using TradingView's news feed. Add a free [Finnhub](https://finnhub.io/register) key in ⚙ SETTINGS to get live headlines that refresh every 60 seconds, with TICKER / TOP / FX / CRYPTO / M&A tabs and a rough ▲/▼ tone tag based on keywords.
- **HEAT**: market map of the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, all US stocks, the DAX or crypto. Tile size is market cap and colour is % change.
- **EQS**: TradingView's screener for US stocks, crypto or FX, with filters and column sets.
- **AI analyst**: ask questions in plain English. Claude gets the symbol on screen, plus the live quote and recent headlines if you added a Finnhub key, and can search the web. Add your own [Anthropic API key](https://platform.claude.com/settings/keys) in ⚙ SETTINGS (or the AI screen's SETUP panel). You pay Anthropic per question, and the screen shows the estimated cost of each answer. The default model is Claude Opus 5.5; Sonnet 5.5 costs about half as much and Haiku 5.5 far less. Answers are information, not financial advice.

- **Analyst note and score** (MAIN, left): a rule-based 0–100 score and plain-English summary. Crypto uses Binance daily candles (EMAs, RSI, MACD, 1-year range, 30 days vs BTC); US stocks use Finnhub (52-week range, 5-day/13-week/26-week returns, strength vs the S&P 500, analyst consensus, next earnings).
- **Live quote strip** above the chart: the price ticking in real time with the day's change and a sparkline. Crypto works with no key (Binance, every 10 s); stocks and FX use Finnhub (quotes every 60 s, plus real-time trades over its websocket).
- **My list**: `ADD TSLA` / `DEL TSLA` edit the watchlist shown in the monitor and the ticker strip.
- **GRID**: 2 or 4 charts at once, either one symbol on several timeframes or several symbols (`GRID 4 NVDA SPY QQQ SMH`).
- **RES**: earnings calendar (your symbols, the largest companies, or everything), analyst ratings, and insider trades. Needs a free Finnhub key.
- **ORDER FLOW**: who is pushing the price. Buyers' share of traded value, net flow, cumulative volume delta against price, large trades, and plain-English readings (aggressive buying, absorption by passive sellers, and so on) for 1m/5m/15m/1h windows. For crypto, the live order book from Binance: spread, bid/ask imbalance within 0.5/1/2%, the biggest walls and a depth chart. For US stocks, Finnhub trades (buy/sell estimated by the tick rule) and 13F institutional holders with market makers (Citadel, Susquehanna, Virtu, Jane Street…) tagged, where your Finnhub plan includes them. A FLOW chip next to the live price shows the 5-minute buy share, and the AI analyst receives the flow summary. Market makers' actual positions are private; this shows the liquidity they post and the trades that happen.
- **PORT**: portfolio with live value, profit and loss, today's change and weights. `BUY AAPL 10 @ 180`, `SELL AAPL 5`, or use the form.
- **ALRT**: price alerts (`ALERT BTCUSD > 70000`) with a notification, a sound and, optionally, an AI explanation of the move; plus a position-size calculator. Alerts are checked while the page is open.
- **Quick AI questions**: one-tap buttons in the AI screen (explain this move, bull vs bear, latest earnings, levels and catalysts, vs sector).
- **Installable app**: click ⤓ INSTALL APP (Chrome/Edge), or on iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen with its own icon.

- **Backtest upgrades**: trend filters (only trade with the 200 EMA, or with the daily trend), a Monte Carlo risk check (2,000 reshuffled runs: typical and bad-luck drawdown, chance the next trades lose money) and APPROVE THIS SETUP when the edge holds out-of-sample. The scanner marks approved setups TESTED (with an "Only tested setups" filter), and an untested setup can't grade A.
- **TAIL RISK** (`TAIL BTCUSD`, `VAR`, `HILL`): how fat a coin's loss tail is, and whether a bell-curve VaR would have held up. Shows sample stats (σ, skew, excess kurtosis, Jarque–Bera, worst day in σ), the Hill tail index α̂ with a 95% range and a plain-English reading, today's 99% and 99.5% VaR and expected shortfall for Gaussian, Historical and Hill/EVT (Weissman extrapolation), and a rolling backtest where each day's VaR is fitted only on the previous 250 or 500 days, scored with Kupiec's proportion-of-failures test. Charts: losses against the three 99% VaR lines, a log-log tail plot (power law vs bell curve) and a Hill plot with its confidence band; k is picked automatically from the most stable 2–6% stretch or set by hand. Crypto only on the free plan (Binance daily history).
- **QUANT** (`QUANT BTCUSD`, `QUANT BTCUSD 4H`, `PROB`, `GARCH`, `REGIME`): bank-style models on one screen, on daily or 4-hour bars. Crypto uses Binance (spot, futures) and Deribit (options); US stocks work with a free Twelve Data key. The models run in a background Web Worker, so the page never freezes.
  - **Buy/sell probability:** a logistic regression on vol-scaled momentum, z-score, RSI, volatility regime, volume surprise and distance to the 20-bar high/low. It is trained walk-forward (re-fitted every 20 bars on past data only) with a ridge penalty, and graded out-of-sample on Brier skill, hit rate, calibration, and a Sharpe and equity curve after 0.2% costs. It shows BUY BIAS or SELL BIAS only when that record proves an edge; otherwise it says NO PROVEN EDGE. Buttons: **→ TRADE FORM** (stop 2σ over the horizon, target 2R, size from your risk rule, half size in a stressed regime) and **PAPER TRADE IT**.
  - **Live track record:** every model call (QUANT screen, scanner, hourly background check of My list) is saved on closed bars and graded once its horizon has passed. It shows the hit rate, the record of BUY/SELL bias calls, Brier skill and recent calls, per symbol and overall. This record only counts calls made before the outcome was known.
  - **Watch My list (hourly while the site is open):** auto paper-trades proven signals (stop 2σ, target 2R, time exit after the horizon, closed automatically), and alerts by toast and browser notification when the regime turns stressed, breakdown odds go above 50% within 5 bars, or a proven model flips.
  - **Chance of breakdown/breakout:** 5,000 filtered-historical-simulation paths (GARCH volatility plus the market's own shocks, no drift) give the odds of touching support/resistance within 5, 10 and 20 bars, plus a price cone. Validated against the Broadie–Glasserman barrier formula.
  - **Volatility and regime:** GARCH(1,1) by maximum likelihood, and a 2-state Gaussian hidden Markov model (Baum–Welch) for the real-time probability of the stressed regime.
  - **Trend or mean reversion:** Hurst exponent (Anis–Lloyd corrected R/S), Lo–MacKinlay variance ratios with robust p-values, and the mean-reversion half-life.
  - **Liquidity:** traded value, Amihud illiquidity, Roll implied spread, the live spread and depth, the cost of a $10k/$100k/$1M market order from walking the order book, and Kyle's lambda from live trades.
  - **Futures (Binance, crypto):** funding rate (annualised and vs its 30-day range), open interest vs price over 7 and 30 days with a plain reading, the retail and top-trader long/short ratios, and estimated liquidation clusters (new hourly open interest spread over 10–100× leverage, minus levels already traded through). Binance Futures is blocked in some countries, including the US.
  - **Options vs model (Deribit, BTC and ETH):** the expiry nearest 30 days; risk-neutral odds of closing below support or above resistance (read from the smile, so skew is included) next to our model's odds; ATM implied volatility vs GARCH; the expected move; 10%-OTM skew; put/call open interest; and a smile chart. Validated against Black–Scholes on a flat smile.
  - **Position size:** 15% volatility targeting, quarter Kelly from the model's out-of-sample record (0% without a proven edge), and half size in the stressed regime.
  - **Settings for quant.pine:** the fitted GARCH and regime parameters, ready to copy into TradingView.
  - **Elsewhere:** the SCANNER shows P(UP 5D) and REGIME columns; the trade checklist adds "Quant model agrees" and "Market regime is calm"; TODAY has a QUANT CALLS card.
- **BOT: paper trading bot** (`BOT`, `BOT ON`, `BOT OFF`): opens paper trades by itself as soon as a setup appears, with no click needed.
  - **What it trades:** scanner setups on closed daily and 4-hour bars (checked every minute), live breakouts of the 20-day high or low in the trend's direction (checked every 15 seconds on live prices), and proven QUANT signals. You choose which setup types it may take (breakout, pullback, volume spike, score, EMA ribbon cross, EMA ribbon pullback), A or A+B grades, TESTED-only, and longs only or shorts too.
  - **Risk limits:** risk per trade (% of the paper account), max open trades, max new trades a day, a daily loss limit that pauses it until tomorrow, a minimum reward:risk from the actual fill, one trade per market, no leverage, and skip or half size in a stressed regime.
  - **Exits run by themselves:** fixed 2R target, ATR trailing stop, half at 1R with the stop to breakeven, or ribbon flip (price crosses the 22 EMA of the ribbon). QUANT trades also have a time exit.
  - **Screen:** status tiles (P&L today and overall, win rate, profit factor, average R, drawdown), open bot trades with a CLOSE button, the equity curve, and an activity log with every trade and every skipped setup and why. CLOSE ALL & STOP is the kill switch. Bot trades land in the JOURNAL tagged BOT.
  - **Paper only:** it fills at the live price plus slippage, and runs while the site is open. It places no real orders and stores no exchange keys.
- **EMA ribbon setups** (BACKTEST, scanner, bot): *EMA ribbon cross* (EMA 8 crosses EMA 22 with RSI on the same side of 50 and the close beyond EMA 8) and *EMA ribbon pullback* (a fully stacked 8–22 ribbon, price dips to its middle and closes back beyond EMA 8). New BACKTEST exits: *Ribbon flip* and *Half at 1R, rest on ribbon flip*.
- **Ribbon OPTIMIZER** (BACKTEST → ⚙ OPTIMIZE RIBBON, or `OPT BTCUSD`): tries about 2,400 EMA-ribbon settings per timeframe on 1h, 4h and daily.
  - **What it varies:** entry (cross, pullback, or both), ribbon speed and tightness, stop (× ATR), exit (fixed target, ribbon flip, or half at 1R + ribbon flip), trend filter and direction.
  - **How it ranks:** by in-sample results only (average R × √trades), keeping settings with at least your minimum trades a month. Each pick is then checked on held-back data against your goal (default +5% of the account, 15+ trades, profit factor 1.2+). Costs are included throughout.
  - **Buttons:** LOAD opens a row in the backtester, APPROVE hands it to the paper bot, and PINE copies the matching TradingView inputs.
  - **Caution:** it says when the goal wasn't met, and warns that choosing a row by its out-of-sample numbers makes them partly luck.
- **Bot: approved strategies.** The paper bot runs every approved strategy with its exact settings: setup, ribbon, stop, target or ribbon-flip exit, trend filter, direction, timeframe and max bars. It checks on the last closed bar every minute and sizes each trade by your bot risk rules.
- **tradingview/ema_ribbon_strategy.pine (v2):** the upgraded "Price-Hugging EMA Ribbon + RSI" strategy. v2 adds continuation entries and both directions by default (more trades), a pause after a losing streak, daily-loss and max-drawdown stops, a goal check (trades per month and net % in-sample and out-of-sample), and a bot connector. The connector auto-trades through TradingView alerts ("Order fills only", message `{{strategy.order.alert_message}}`) with 3Commas Signal Bot, PineConnector (MT4/MT5) or custom JSON messages.
  - **Realism:** commission and slippage, risk-based sizing instead of 100% of equity, ATR or behind-the-ribbon stops with R targets re-anchored to the real fill, and a cooldown after exits.
  - **Exits and entries:** ribbon-flip, ATR-trail and half-at-1R exits, and pullback entries as well as crosses.
  - **Filters:** a non-repainting higher-timeframe trend filter, plus optional ADX, ribbon-width and volume filters.
  - **Testing and alerts:** an in-sample vs out-of-sample table with a verdict, alertconditions, and JSON alert() messages for webhooks.
- **Portfolio risk** (PORTFOLIO → RISK ANALYSIS, or `CORR`): for holdings plus open real trades, it shows a correlation map, 1-day 99% VaR (historical and bell-curve) and expected shortfall, the diversification ratio, each position's share of risk, risk-parity weights, and warnings when two positions are really the same bet.
- **Twelve Data key (free, ⚙ Settings):** daily and 4-hour US stock history for QUANT, TAIL RISK, BACKTEST, the scanner and portfolio risk (8 calls a minute; calls queue and longer histories are reused).
- **tradingview/quant.pine:** GARCH volatility, the hidden-Markov stressed-regime probability (background turns red), breakdown/breakout odds to the 20-bar low/high, Hurst and variance ratio in a table, and four alerts (regime turned stressed or calm, breakdown or breakout odds above your threshold) that TradingView runs 24/7. Paste the fitted numbers from QUANT section 10 into its inputs.
- **SCANNER**: checks every symbol in My list against the backtester's setups on the last closed daily and 4-hour bars, plus trend-pullback candidates. Each setup gets an entry, a 1.5× ATR stop, a 2R target, a size from your risk rule and an A/B/C grade with the reasons it isn't an A. LOG fills the journal form. "Alert me on new A-grade setups" scans every 5 minutes in the background and alerts once per setup per day. Crypto works free; stocks need price history (paid Finnhub plan).
- **BACKTEST**: test a setup (breakout, pullback to EMA20, volume spike, score 70/30) on up to 5,000 bars of Binance history for crypto, with ATR stops, R targets, fixed / trailing / half-at-1R exits, commission and slippage. Entries fill on the bar after the signal; if stop and target are both hit in one bar, the stop counts. The last 20–40% of the data is held back as out-of-sample, and a verdict says whether the edge holds there. COMPARE SETUPS and COMPARE TIMEFRAMES rank the options by out-of-sample results. For stocks, use `tradingview/terminal_strategy.pine` in TradingView's Strategy Tester (same rules, with an in-sample / out-of-sample table on the chart).
- **JRNL (trade journal)**: log trades (`LOG LONG NVDA 10 @ 130 STOP 125 TARGET 145`, or the form, with setup and reasons), close them (`CLOSE NVDA @ 140`, or at the live price), and see net P&L, win rate, profit factor, average win and loss, expectancy, average R, max drawdown, an equity curve, and results by setup and symbol. Risk rules (daily loss limit, max risk per trade, max trades per day) show a red banner across the terminal when broken. EXPORT CSV for spreadsheets; AI REVIEW asks the analyst to critique your trades.
- **Pre-trade checklist and grade**: as you fill in the journal's NEW TRADE form, seven checks run: trend on the daily and 4-hour charts, order flow, reward at least 2× risk, risk within your rule, no earnings or high-impact US release in 24 hours, live price data, and the market mood. A = nothing fails or looks weak (up to two checks may be unconfirmed, marked ?), B = nothing fails, C = something fails. C-grade trades need a second click to log. ATR STOP fills a stop 1.5× daily ATR away and a 2R target. Every trade keeps its grade, and the journal shows results by grade.
- **Smarter exits**: choose an exit plan per trade: fixed target, trailing stop (1.5× daily ATR, only ever tightens) or half at 1R with the rest moved to breakeven. Open trades are watched against live prices, with alerts when the stop, trailing stop, 1R or target is hit. HALF closes half a position. R is always measured against the stop planned at entry. The insights compare exit plans.
- **Paper trading**: tick "Paper trade" (or `PAPER LONG BTCUSD 0.01 STOP 59000 TARGET 62000`) to open a trade at the live price plus 0.05% slippage; closing fills the same way. Filter the journal by REAL, PAPER or both.
- **Journal insights**: what skipping C-grade trades would have saved, A-grade results, the best exit plan, worst hour and day to trade, results after two losses in a row, holding losers longer than winners, trades without stops, and average win vs average loss.
- **Process tracker** (top of the journal): 1 backtest → 2 paper trade (20+ trades, PF ≥ 1.2) → 3 small real money → 4 weekly review → 5 size up (30+ real trades, PF ≥ 1.2), with warnings when a step is skipped.
- **Upcoming events** (CHARTS sidebar): market mood, next earnings date and high-impact US releases in the next 24 hours where the Finnhub plan includes the economic calendar.
- **MOOD light** (header): risk-on / mixed / risk-off for stocks (SPY returns and 52-week position, Finnhub) and crypto (BTC daily trend and 30-day return, Binance).
- **Backup**: BACKUP ALL saves your watchlist, alerts, portfolio, journal and settings to a file (API keys are left out); RESTORE loads it on another device or browser.
- **Reliability**: lights in the header show each data source's state (TradingView, Binance, Finnhub quotes, Finnhub live feed); every live price carries a LIVE / DELAYED / STALE / CLOSED tag (also in the portfolio and alerts); crypto prices are cross-checked against Coinbase; an offline banner appears when the connection drops; the calculator warns before using a delayed price.

- **Speed and stability**: MAIN's TradingView panels load only when MAIN is opened; redraws pause while the tab is hidden (prices and alerts keep running); every timer is guarded, so an error in one feature shows a short note instead of breaking the page; TODAY shows a setup checklist until the key pieces are in place; a short note after each update says what changed.

API keys are stored only in your browser and are sent only to their own provider (Finnhub or Anthropic). Don't save them on a shared computer, and set a monthly spend limit in the Claude Console.

The chart is TradingView's embeddable widget, which can't load custom Pine scripts. To use `terminal.pine`, open it on tradingview.com.

### Hosting on GitHub Pages

`.github/workflows/pages.yml` publishes the `web/` folder whenever it changes on `main`. One-time setup:

1. In the repo on GitHub, go to **Settings → Pages** and set **Source** to **GitHub Actions**.
2. Merge this branch into `main`. The workflow runs and the site appears at the address above. You can also run it by hand from the **Actions** tab.
