    INSTITUTIONS & MARKET MAKERS (13F)

    Market makers don't publish their positions. What you can see: the liquidity they post (the order book), whether buyers or sellers are paying up (trade flow), and the quarterly 13F holdings of big firms, which include market makers like Citadel Securities, Susquehanna, Virtu and Jane Street, filed up to 45 days after each quarter. Walls in the book can be pulled before price reaches them. This is information, not a trade signal.

    CALC · POSITION SIZE

    Position size— Position value— Money at risk— Risk per unit— Reward : risk— Profit at target—

    Size = (account × risk %) ÷ (entry − stop). If the position is larger than the account, it needs margin.

    SCAN · SETUP SCANNER
    BACKTEST · PROVE THE STRATEGY FIRSTCrypto here (Binance history). Stocks: tradingview/terminal_strategy.pine on TradingView.
    Pick a crypto symbol and a setup, then press RUN. The test enters on the bar after each signal, checks the stop before the target when both are hit in the same bar, and charges commission and slippage on every fill. The last part of the history is held back as out-of-sample: tune the settings while looking only at the in-sample results, then trust the setup only if the out-of-sample results hold up too.
