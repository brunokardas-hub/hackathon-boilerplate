Position size— Position value— Money at risk— Risk per unit— Reward : risk— Profit at target—

Size = (account × risk %) ÷ (entry − stop). If the position is larger than the account, it needs margin.

Data lights (top right): green working, amber slow or limited, red down, grey not in use. Click one for details. Every live price carries a tag: LIVE, DELAYED, STALE or CLOSED. Don't trade on a STALE price. For alerts that run 24/7, see the TradingView alerts guide.

Install as an app: on a computer, click ⤓ INSTALL APP at the top right (Chrome or Edge). On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari. On Android, tap ⋮ → Install app in Chrome.

News: without a key the panel shows TradingView's news feed. With a free Finnhub key it shows Finnhub headlines with a rough keyword tone tag (▲/▼). That tag is a word match, not analysis.
